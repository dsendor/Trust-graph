# Weekend 3 attribution, round 2: twelve more P5 2014 bets

Companion to `actors-round2-p5.csv` (95 rows: 12 committee, 57 person, 26 agency; 88
confident, 7 guess). Written 2026-10-02. Same columns and conventions as
`actors-draft.csv`; existing actor_ids (`committee-p5-2014`, `steve-ritz`, `agency-doe`,
`agency-nsf`, `steven-kahn`, `tony-tyson`, `lia-merminga`, `bonnie-fleming`) are reused
exactly. The P5 panel-member rows with blank bet_id are not repeated. Named people stay in
the draft files only (CLAUDE.md rule 7).

Bets covered: mu2e, muon-g-2, hl-lhc, lhc-phase-1, pip-ii, lsst, dm-g2, pingu, mice,
chips, lar1, sbn. For each: one committee row, one chair row, one DOE and/or NSF funder
row per bets.csv, agency actions with dates where a readable page states them, and three
to seven champions (lead author or spokesperson plus project head, later leaders labelled).

## Cross-links worth knowing before the trust graph

- Bonnie Fleming sat on the P5 2014 panel and was a contact person for the LAr1-ND
  proposal (arXiv:1309.7987) that P5 declined, and MicroBooNE co-spokesperson in the SBN
  portfolio P5 recommended instead.
- Lia Merminga sat on the P5 2014 panel (as TRIUMF) and was PIP-II project director
  2018-2022, then Fermilab director.
- Steve Kahn is DESC co-spokesperson in 2012 (arXiv:1211.0310), LSST Director from 2013
  (astro2010-lsst guess row), and an author of the 2010 LSST camera design paper.
- Francis Halzen (P5 member) is in the PINGU LoI author list; his IceCube PI title is not
  on any page read.
- Steve Nahn is US project manager for both the CMS Phase-1 upgrade (2019) and the
  DOE-funded CMS HL-LHC upgrade (2025).
- Ornella Palamara and David Schmitz carry over from LAr1-ND contact persons (2013) to
  SBND co-spokespersons (2015-2024).
- Steve Ritz as LSST camera project scientist (general knowledge) was not checked and is
  not in the file; it would be a direct conflict on p5-2014-lsst if confirmed.

### p5-2014-mu2e
Snowmass white paper arXiv:1307.1168 (2013-07-03), lead author Kevin Knoepfel, authors
include Bernstein, Glenzinski, Miller, Miscetti, Ray. TDR arXiv:1501.05241 (2015-01-21,
263 authors, no roles flagged). Fermilab news 2020-10-12: Miller co-spokesperson since
2007; Bernstein 2007-2014, back from March 2020; Glenzinski 2014 to March 2020.
2020-11-11: Ron Ray project manager. 2023-05-19: Miscetti elected co-spokesperson with
Bernstein. Agency: DOE funder and the December 2022 rebaseline (p5-2014-agency.csv,
FY2025 request). Not verified: whether Glenzinski's 2014 takeover preceded the May 2014
report; Ray as PM in 2014 (snippet of the 2014-12 Fermilab project-management item).
